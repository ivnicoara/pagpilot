What you'll do

A little bit of everything, with one clear priority

This is a generalist role. Your week can include a partner call, a support audit, a competitor teardown and a new retention email. The most important part is below.

Priority #1

Win back users who stop paying

Our churn is high right now and we do almost nothing with people who cancel. You'll own this. Design the win-back flows, launch them, measure them and keep improving them.

  • Find out why people leave: cancel surveys, calls, usage data
  • Build win-back sequences across email, in-app, SMS and calls
  • Test offers, timing and messaging, and report what works
  • Earn a bonus as a percentage of the revenue you recover
Day 0User cancels → exit survey: "What didn't work?"
Day 2Personal email based on the reason they gave
Day 7"Here's what's new" + 1 free month to come back
Day 21Call or WhatsApp for high-value accounts
WinUser reactivates → you earn % of recovered MRR

Example only. You'll design the real flows.

Run partner calls

Get on calls with the agencies, affiliates and partners we work with. Check if they bring results and agree on clear next steps.

Improve reviews and support

Optimize how our support agents ask for reviews in chat. Read conversations, check they talk to users well, and coach them on what to do better.

Close API and white-label deals

Find and pitch partners who want to use Store Pilot through our API or sell it under their own brand.

Watch the competition

Track what other AI store and page builders launch, how they price, and what users say about them.

Bring feature ideas

Turn what you hear from users, partners and competitors into concrete feature proposals for the product team.

Build your own tools

Need a dashboard, an automation or a small internal platform? Build it yourself with AI, no-code or code, without waiting for developers.
