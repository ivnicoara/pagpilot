# Job video script: Business Development Manager

**Speaker:** Valentin, HR Manager at Page Pilot
**Length:** about 2 to 2.5 minutes (roughly 330 words)
**Where it goes:** the video box at the top of job.pagepilot.ai

**Recording tips**
- Talk to the camera like you're explaining the job to a friend. Don't read word for word; use this as a guide.
- Film horizontally (16:9) in a quiet room, with light in front of you.
- The lines in *[brackets]* are optional screen recordings (B-roll) to cut in while you talk.

---

### 1. Hook (0:00–0:15)

Hey, I'm Valentin, HR Manager at Page Pilot.

We're looking for a Business Development Manager. This person can do a little bit of everything, understands SaaS and e-commerce, and doesn't wait to be told what to do.

If that sounds like you, watch the next two minutes.

### 2. Who we are (0:15–0:40)

*[Screen: pagepilot.ai, paste a product link, the page generates]*

We're a fully remote team, and since 2023 we've built two AI products for Shopify sellers.

**Page Pilot** turns a product link into a high-converting product page in under a minute. More than 400,000 merchants have used it.

*[Screen: store.pagepilot.ai]*

**Store Pilot** builds a complete Shopify store with AI in about ten minutes, with winning products already loaded.

We're a small remote team with a lot of users all over the world, so one good person here makes a real difference.

### 3. The main job (0:40–1:05)

I'll be honest with you. Our biggest problem right now is churn. Many users stop paying, and today we do almost nothing to bring them back.

That's your number one job. Find out why people leave. Build the flows that win them back: emails, in-app messages, offers, even calls. Test them, measure them, improve them.

And that's why the pay is a base salary plus bonuses, including a percentage of the revenue you recover. So the better you are at it, the more you earn.

### 4. Everything else (1:05–1:30)

Besides that, your week will look like this:

- Calls with the partners we work with, to check they bring results and agree on next steps.
- Helping our support team get more reviews in chat, and coaching them to talk to users better.
- Finding partners for our API and white-label version of Store Pilot.
- Watching what competitors do, and bringing us ideas for new features.
- And if you need a dashboard or an automation, you build it yourself with AI or no-code. You don't wait for a developer.

### 5. Who we're looking for (1:30–1:50)

You've worked in or with SaaS, so you know what MRR and churn mean. You understand e-commerce, and it's even better if you've run a Shopify store yourself. You're comfortable jumping between a partner call, a spreadsheet and a support chat on the same day. And you use AI every day, not just to write emails, but to research, analyse and build things faster.

You get a base salary plus bonuses based on your results.

### 6. How to apply (1:50–2:20)

*[Screen: the apply page, scroll through the steps]*

To apply, click the button on this page. It takes about fifteen minutes.

The most important part is a short Loom video, 3 to 5 minutes. Tell us who you are, show us one result you're proud of with real numbers, and then open Page Pilot or Store Pilot, share your screen, and give us one idea to win back users who cancel.

One more thing: read the whole job page. There's a code word at the bottom, and the form will ask you for it.

I watch every video myself. Looking forward to seeing yours.
